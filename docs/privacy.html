Privacy Policy

MiniTally

MiniTally is designed to be a simple, private counter app. Your counters and settings stay on your device.

Data Collection

  • MiniTally does not collect personal information.
  • MiniTally does not use analytics.
  • MiniTally does not track you across apps or websites.
  • MiniTally does not require an account.

Local App Data

  • Counters, saved counters, colours, modes, layouts, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • This data is used only to provide the app features you choose to use.
  • No MiniTally counter data is sent to the developer.

Voice, Sounds, and Feedback

  • Voice feedback uses system text-to-speech features.
  • Haptics, vibration, and click sounds are handled on device.
  • These features do not send your counter values to the developer.

Support

If you contact support by email, your email address and message are used only to reply to your request.
