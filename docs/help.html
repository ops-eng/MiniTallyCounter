MiniTally Counter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

MiniTally Counter is designed to be a simple, private tally counter. The app does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

  • MiniTally Counter does not collect personal information.
  • The app does not use analytics.
  • The app does not use advertising identifiers.
  • The app does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No account is required to use the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store your counters, saved counter names, colours, layout choices, and settings locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Voice, Sound, and Haptics

MiniTally Counter can use system features such as haptics, sound, vibration, and text-to-speech. These features run on your device using Apple system services.

Third-Party Services

MiniTally Counter does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.
